Decimal places apply to per-line amounts (headline totals stay whole dollars). Margin/GST take effect everywhere the margin ladder is computed — Quote Summary, Dashboard, print & Excel exports. Overheads/contingency seed new projects only; existing projects keep their own values.

App behaviour

Turning off delete confirmation makes every ✕ Remove a single, immediate click — there is no undo. Auto-publish keeps the Cost Planner fed live from Estimates' Quantity Register; turn it off if you'd rather it not update automatically.

Estimates — defaults for new takeoffs

These seed the Defaults & Rules page and element cards of every NEW Estimates takeoff/element. A takeoff already saved keeps its own values (still editable per project on its Defaults & Rules tab). The lap toggle pre-ticks every "Include lap allowance (%)" checkbox on newly added elements — you can still untick any of them per item.
