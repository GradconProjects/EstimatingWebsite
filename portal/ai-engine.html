Quality checks

When a Quotes project reaches Completed Estimating the checks run on it: subcontract quote rows with no figure, rates past their validity, rate drift since the project was pinned, kg/m³ of steel and m²/m³ of formwork outside the usual bands, concrete against the measures, crew days against the handover bands, duplicated elements, and $/m² against Gradcon's own submitted projects — then the AI explains what looks wrong and asks for a manual review. This panel arrives in the next build; the takeoff and specification readers above are live now.
