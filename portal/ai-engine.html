Quality checks

Pick a Quotes project and run the checks: subcontract quote rows with no figure, rates past their validity, rate drift since the project was pinned (with its cost), kg/m³ of steel and m²/m³ of formwork outside the usual bands, concrete against the recorded measures, crew days against the handover bands, duplicated or empty elements, part-entered rows, and $/m² against Gradcon's own submitted projects. Every finding is a fact from the costing library; the AI then explains what looks wrong and what to look at. Nothing on the project is changed — tick a finding to record that it was reviewed.
